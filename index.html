Questions recruiters ask

Short answers before you write to us.

How do I contact a student?

Open their card and use the LinkedIn button, or write to the Placement Committee and we will coordinate interviews and campus or virtual drives for you.

Which batch is this?

The BMS (undergraduate) batch of IIM Sirmaur. Each card shows the student's own domains, hard skills and preferred locations once they confirm them.

Is everything on a card confirmed by the student?

Details marked Sample are placeholders while the batch finishes submitting their profiles. Use the Student-confirmed details filter to see only profiles the student has completed.

Does the portal track me?

No. There are no cookies, analytics or third-party requests. Your shortlist is saved only in your browser and never sent anywhere.

How do I share a filtered view with my team?

Click Copy link to this view. The link reopens the same filters, sort order and layout.
