Be easy to find.

Recruiters see this portal first. Make sure your profile is complete, and make sure the people who find you on LinkedIn can find this page too.

Three steps

Takes about ten minutes. Do it before the Placement Committee sends the portal link to companies.

  1. Submit your details. Fill in the profile form from the Placement Committee: CGPA, domains (a primary one plus up to two more), three to five skills, clubs and committees, any internship, preferred locations and your LinkedIn URL.
    Form link: to be added by the committee
  2. Polish your LinkedIn. The portal sends recruiters straight to it. Use a clear photo, a headline that names your target domain, and an About section with two or three lines on what you want to do and what you have done.
  3. Pin the portal on LinkedIn. On your profile choose Add profile section → Featured → Add a link and paste the portal address. Then share the post below so alumni and recruiters in your network see it.

Ready-to-post LinkedIn text

Edit the highlighted parts so it sounds like you, then post it.

Proud to share that the IIM Sirmaur BMS batch now has its own talent portal. Based in the foothills of Himachal, we know it's hard to see all of us in one place. So we built one: filter by domain (consulting, founder's office, marketing, finance, operations, supply chain, sales and more), by CGPA and by skills, and go straight to our LinkedIn profiles. I'm looking for roles in [YOUR DOMAIN]. You can find me here: [PORTAL LINK] #IIMSirmaur #BMS #Internships #StudentsForHire

Replace [PORTAL LINK] once the site is live.
